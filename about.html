About the creator

Created to preserve and share Sámi cultural research.

This digital archive was created by Rachel Zhang, a senior from Basis International School Shenzhen, to preserve traditional Sámi culture and serve as a centralized resource for future scholars.

About Rachel

A student researcher with roots in Finland

Rachel was born in Finland and has developed a deep interest in researching Sámi culture, literature, language, and history. Her connection to Finland helped inspire her curiosity about the Sámi people and the importance of making Sámi research easier to find.

She is currently studying in China at Basis International School China. Through this project, Rachel brings together her academic interests, personal background, and commitment to preserving access to Indigenous cultural knowledge.

Background

Why this project was created

During her own academic journey, Rachel encountered numerous challenges in locating scattered resources about the Sámi. Because formal Sámi academia only emerged in the late 20th century, and because the culture's profound history was carried through rich oral traditions rather than written texts prior to the 1900s, vital resources are often difficult to track down.

This website is designed to ease that resource-finding barrier. Rachel built it around an author-centric framework that maps the intellectual landscape through 40 pivotal figures, pairing foundational Sámi authors, poets, and activists with key historians and linguists.

The database links creators directly to their bibliographies, languages, and archival resources. Through this structured curation, Rachel hopes to honor the voices of Sápmi, streamline the research process, and empower the next generation of Indigenous studies.
